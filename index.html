Logistics & Warehousing Case Study

Distribution Hub AS/RS Automation

Challenge

The client's central fulfillment facility suffered from bottlenecked peak-season order processing, high manual labor costs, and a pick error rate exceeding 2.8%, leading to customer churn.

Solution

Led an end-to-end design and installation of Goods-to-Person (GTP) autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) integrated with a customized Warehouse Management System (WMS), intelligent conveyor slotting, and RF scanner pick verification.

Key Results

  • Boosted warehouse order throughput by 45% during peak holiday season.
  • Decreased annual warehouse OPEX by $3.2 Million.
  • Elevated picking accuracy from 97.2% to 99.9%.
Procurement & Sourcing Case Study

Global Component Restructuring & Strategic Sourcing

Challenge

Single-source dependencies in East Asia caused critical production halts during global transport bottlenecks, inflating freight expenditures and lengthening customer lead times.

Solution

Executed global RFQs, evaluated vendor risk indexes, and implemented a nearshoring strategy by onboarding strategic manufacturing partners in Mexico and Eastern Europe with dual-sourcing fallback contracts.

Key Results

  • Unlocked $8.4M in direct bill-of-materials (BOM) savings over 24 months.
  • Reduced average inbound component lead time from 54 days to 32 days.
  • Mitigated single-source supply chain risk across 100% of top revenue product lines.
ERP Optimization Case Study

Enterprise SAP S/4HANA Supply Chain Rollout

Challenge

Legacy ERP systems operated in silos across 6 manufacturing plants, causing inaccurate inventory visibility, manual purchase orders, and inflated holding costs.

Solution

Configured SAP S/4HANA Materials Management (MM) and Production Planning (PP), standardized safety stock parameters, and built real-time executive PowerBI dashboards.

Key Results

  • Reduced working capital tied up in safety stock by 35% ($11M freed up).
  • Achieved real-time end-to-end inventory visibility across all business units.
  • Completed global deployment 6 months ahead of initial timeline with zero downtime.
Logistics Case Study

Fleet Routing & Dynamic Freight Optimization
